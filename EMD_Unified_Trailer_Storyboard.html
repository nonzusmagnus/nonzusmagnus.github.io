Movement & Clarity — Unified Trailer Storyboard

01 / Footballer

Empty stadium + tunnel • Black tee: Movement • White tee: Clarity

Full script

We open in an empty stadium tunnel on the Footballer alone. The Rapper is barely present — a very brief, out-of-focus background pass in hi-vis at the very start of Movement (1–2s max), then gone. The scene then opens with the Footballer and two teammates who are leaving while he stays to put in extra work. He paces, then steps out with ball at feet. In Movement he works cones but touches run heavy, the ball gets away, a long strike disappoints, and he makes the long solo walk to retrieve it. This is a wide-lens shot to show the distance he travels alone — the lonely road and solitude it takes to grow in that period. The transition to Clarity is the Rapper in hi-vis as janitor looking from the benches out toward the Footballer, then switch to the Footballer in white for Clarity. In Clarity he runs the same drill with cleaner touches, quicker decisions and a better strike, allows a small smirk, and jogs the same distance with ease to retrieve the ball and go again. Final shot is the hi-vis Rapper watching him as he packs his stuff to close work.

Dialogue Friend: "We're about to head." Him: "I'm going to stick around." Friends give a cheeky look — this guy — and leave. Rapper (final, packing up): "One day."

Movement beats

  • Tunnel: Rapper 1–2s pass only. Footballer + 2 teammates farewell.
  • Paces, steps out. Heavy cone work, ball away. Long strike disappoints.
  • Wide-lens solo walk — lonely road.

Clarity beats

  • Rapper on benches looks out — cut to white tee. Cleaner, quicker, better strike, smirk.
  • Jogs back with ease, retrieves ball, goes again.
Transition: Rapper's "One day" carries us — we leave with him to the studio.

02 / Rapper

Stadium → bus stop → Simba's studio • Black / White tee

Full script

This segment starts with the Rapper as the janitor in the stadium — headphones in, beat running, as he types lyrics on his phone. This covers his journey from 9-to-5 work to studio sessions, showing consistent effort and utilizing all hours of the day to achieve his dream. Next he packs up, then waits at the bus stop where an idea for the finished song hits. Vest open so the Movement tee is visible. He heads to Simba's real working studio. In Movement he writes, scraps and discards pages; after the block he finally steps into the booth for a quick montage of stopping, restarting and changing flow. For the transition to Clarity the producer resets the energy. In Clarity his delivery comes more naturally and better — he is in flow state, more notorious, locked in with his producer / engineer. He comes out of the booth to confirm the take. It then transitions to the music being played on radio stations, stores, and finally in the tattoo parlor where the next segment begins.

Dialogue Producer: "Let's pause that for a second. Try this beat." Engineer: "I think we got one. But let's go again to make sure."

Movement beats

  • Janitor — headphones, beat, typing lyrics. 9-to-5 to dream.
  • Packs up. Bus stop idea. Vest open. To Simba's studio.
  • Writes, scraps, discards. Booth montage: stop / restart / change flow.

Clarity beats

  • New beat. Natural delivery, flow state. Locked in.
  • Out of booth confirmation. Track spreads to radio / stores.
Transition: track low in the tattoo parlor. Enter Gabi.

03 / Tattoo Artist — Gabi

Dublin 8 store, just reopened • Black / White tee

Full script

Gabi walks into her tattoo store which has just reopened. She wears wired earphones, listening to the previous Rapper's music low. The store is quiet and kind of dim, as she has had troubles beginning and starting over. In Movement she flips the sign, wipes dust and sets up slowly. She sketches expressionist linework, rejects versions and tries again. Needle test buzz. She holds the stencil against skin, pauses, makes a hesitant first pass, then wipes, breathes and adjusts her angle. For the bridge to Clarity the light lifts slightly as day comes in. In Clarity her hand is settled and lines go clean. Machine off. The client — the Content Creator getting a temporary tattoo to mark dedication to craft — looks genuinely happy. She catches it with a small smirk and goes back to cleaning down for the next set-up.

Dialogue Client (soft): "You sure it's ready?" Gabi: "Almost." Gabi (quiet): "Give me a sec." Client: "Take your time." Gabi: "Got it."

Movement beats

  • Enters reopened store, wired earphones. Quiet, dim.
  • Slow set-up. Sketch, reject. Needle buzz. Stencil test, hesitant pass.

Clarity beats

  • Light lifts. Clean passes, machine off. Temp tattoo revealed, happy.
  • Smirk. Cleans down, next set-up.
Transition: Creator leaves the chair — follow him home.

04 / Content Creator

Bedroom setup, ring light • Black / White tee

Full script

He is introduced at the end of the tattoo artist segment showing his new tattoo. For his own segment he heads back home checking the views on his recent post — 70 likes and 5 comments from supportive friends. Inside he sets up for a live stream. Right before he begins, his African mum comes into the room — dramatic and comedic, speaking Pidgin English — giving out about the dishes not being done and wasting time on the computer instead of reading his book. Dialogue is minimal. He is frustrated and a bit disheartened. She leaves the room. He takes a sigh but pushes on with the content and the live stream. As he turns to the computer and the camera is now in front of him, we transition to Clarity in the white tee. He brightens as he sees the support of his community in chat and begins responding to messages.

Dialogue Creator: "So guys, check out my new tattoo." Mum (Pidgin): "Why you dey always waste your time for this computer when you fit dey read your book?"

Movement beats

  • Tag at shop. Home — checks post: 70 likes, 5 comments.
  • Sets up stream. Mum interruption — dishes beat. Frustrated, sighs.

Clarity beats

  • Turn to camera = white tee. Brightens at chat, responds to messages.
Transition: stream plays on a phone — reveal the Boxer watching on his way to the gym.

05 / Boxer

Boxing gym • Black / White tee

Full script

The Boxer is introduced at the end of the content creator segment as one of the friends commenting in the chat box. It transitions to him walking toward his training boxing gym. He makes his way in and trains with his coach but he's just off — timing loose, unfocused. The coach is frustrated and tells him his head's not completely in it. We see him mentally frustrated, taking a second, breathing and resetting in the corner. The transition to Clarity shows him a lot sharper, more focused and dialed in — transitioned to a professional fighter. Combinations land clean, footwork is settled, rhythm with the coach is restored. Belts and his star on the boxing gym walls show his progress. He puts it down and goes back to work.

Dialogue Coach (frustrated): "Your head's not in it."

Movement beats

  • Tag in chat, walk to gym. Pads off, timing loose.
  • Coach call-out. Corner reset — frustrated, breathes.

Clarity beats

  • Sharper, dialed in. Clean combos, settled footwork.
  • Belts / star wall. Back to bag — professional.
Close loop: back to work. Optional buzz — Creator live again. "I see it a little clearer now. Keep going."
