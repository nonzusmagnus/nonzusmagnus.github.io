Concept 01 — Working Script

Pick Up
The Phone

A flip phone falls out of a clear sky and lands at three friends' feet. Whoever opens it doesn't get pulled anywhere, they glitch out on the spot, gone in a burst of digital noise, and land somewhere worth being.

Runtime35–45s
FormatHorizontal
Cast3 Leads
LocationsHangout · Arena · Padel Court · Bowling Alley
Act I — Something Falls
01 Out of a Clear Sky Ext. Sky — Cold Open
Action
We open cold, high above the city. A single flip phone tumbles end over end through a break in the clouds, catching light as it falls. just the object, falling, alone against the sky.
Camera
Drone/aerial POV looking straight down or a slow drift alongside the phone as it drops. Let it hang in frame — this is the hook, give it a beat before cutting.
Hard cut down to street level
02 Right at Their Feet Ext. Rooftop / Stoop — Night
Action
Close on three faces — bored, mid-hangout, half-finished snacks between them. Then pull out to wide: the phone drops with a hard thud right in the middle of them, skids to a stop, glowing faintly at the hinge. Three heads turn down at once.
Dialogue
"...that just fall out of the sky?" / "Only one way to find out."
Person 1 picks it up, flips it open
Act II — First Glitch
03 Gone in a Glitch Rooftop → Super Social Arena
Action
Person 1 picks up the phone hesitantly and is sucked into the phone digitally. They are then transported to Super Social Arena. The camera whips and they run to play one of the games.
Camera
Whip pan as Person 1 gets pulled in — digital suction effect, then a handheld sprint into the arena floor.
Cut to empty rooftop
04 Two Left Ext. Rooftop — Continuous
Action
Camera cuts back to Person 2 and Person 3. Person 2 is excited and picks up the phone as Person 3 is reluctant.
Dialogue
Person 2: *Shrugs shoulders *"May as well". Person 3: "think i'll pass."
Person 2 grabs the phone
05 Return of Serve Rooftop → Padel Court
Action
Ends the scene with person 2 hitting the padel ball into the camera.
Camera
POV rig at the back wall for the ball-to-lens hit; datamosh flicker on the landing beat before the swing.
Impact whites out — resets on Person 3
Act III — The Hold-Out
06 Last One Standing Ext. Rooftop — Continuous
Action
Finally cuts back to Person 3 alone, looking left to right, breathes then opens the phone. He arrives at Leisureplex Bowling, walks backward then rose the bowling ball and hits the strike.
Performance Note
Play the hesitation real — this is the audience's own resistance being talked down on camera.
Glitches out, last time
07 Strike Rooftop → Leisure Plex Lanes
Action
Camera follows the bowling ball as it hits a strike and transitions to the end card.
Camera
Low lane-level tracking shot on the ball; ramp to real-time on pin contact for maximum snap.
Pins scatter into the end card
End Card

Three Doors. One Phone.

Falling pins wipe into the closing card — the three worlds we just visited, now laid out as real, bookable events.
