Concept 01 — Working Script

The Signal
Answers First

A dead flip phone finds three friends bored of their own routine — and pulls each of them, one by one, into something worth showing up for.

Runtime~45–60s
FormatVertical / Social Cut
Cast3 Leads
LocationsHangout · Arena · Padel Court · Bowling Alley
Act I — The Lull
01 Nothing's Happening Ext. Rooftop / Stoop — Night
Action
Three friends are slumped around a rooftop ledge, phones long forgotten. Half-finished snacks, string lights buzzing. The conversation has looped back to the same complaint for the third time tonight.
Dialogue (overlap, loose)
"Same four walls, same three options." / "We need something to actually happen for once."
Wind kicks up — SFX: low rumble
02 Something Lands Same — Continuous
Action
A battered flip phone cartwheels across the rooftop, caught in the same gust, and skids to a stop at Person 1's feet. It's glowing faintly at the hinge — not plugged into anything. Person 1 picks it up, turns it over, curious.
Camera
Low insert push-in on the phone as it lands. Cut to Person 1's face — half amused, half unsure.
Beat: they flip it open
Act II — First Pull
03 Pulled Through Rooftop → Super Social Arena
Action
The screen floods with light. Person 1 is yanked forward, feet lifting off the rooftop — a hard whip-pan through the phone's screen. Match cut: they land mid-motion, already inside Super Social, gripping a controller, deep in a live Arena match. Crowd noise crashes in.
Camera
Speed-ramped pull, screen-flash whip cut, then a handheld arena shot — energy up, lights up, sound up.
Snap cut back to rooftop
04 Two Left Ext. Rooftop — Continuous
Action
Person 1 is just gone. The phone sits alone on the ledge, still glowing. Person 2 lunges for it immediately, buzzing. Person 3 leans back, arms crossed, already shaking their head.
Dialogue
Person 2: "I'm not asking twice." / Person 3: "Hard pass."
Person 2 grabs the phone
05 Return of Serve Rooftop → Padel Court
Action
Same whip-pull through the screen. Person 2 lands mid-swing on a padel court, already in motion, and drills a return dead into the lens — the ball fills frame, a hard smash-cut on impact.
Camera
POV rig at the back wall for the ball-to-lens hit; whip-pan on impact resets the frame.
Impact whites out — resets on Person 3
Act III — The Hold-Out
06 Last One Standing Ext. Rooftop — Continuous
Action
Person 3, alone now, stares down the phone on the ledge. A long beat of resistance — then a half-smile breaks through. They pick it up before they can talk themselves out of it and flip it open.
Performance Note
Play the hesitation real — this is the audience's own resistance being talked down on camera.
Pulled through, last time
07 Strike Rooftop → Leisure Plex Lanes
Action
Person 3 lands mid-approach at the lanes, releases without missing a step. Slow-motion on the ball's roll, cut to a full-speed pin strike — every pin down, no hesitation left in the frame.
Camera
Low lane-level tracking shot on the ball; ramp to real-time on pin contact for maximum snap.
Pins scatter into the end card
End Card

Three Doors. One Phone.

Falling pins wipe into the closing card — the three worlds we just visited, now laid out as real, bookable events.
