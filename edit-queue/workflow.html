Edit Pipeline

Every job moves through these 7 steps.

In the edit queue Waiting on client or admin
  1. 1

    Booked

    Job created and the client gets their status link.

    Needs deposit + footage to continue
  2. 2

    In queue

    The client sees their place in line and an estimated draft date.

  3. 3

    Editing

    Being cut now.

  4. 4

    Draft sent

    The client watches the draft and sends notes within 24 or 48 hours.

  5. 5

    Revisions

    Notes are applied, then a new draft goes back to step 4.

    ↺ Repeats, with 2 rounds included
  6. 6

    Final ready

    Approved. The balance invoice is sent.

    Needs balance paid to release files
  7. 7

    Delivered

    Files are released and the job is closed.

Queue rules

  • No place in the queue until the deposit and footage are in.
  • Rush jobs go to the front for a fee.
  • Waiting on a client never holds up the queue.
