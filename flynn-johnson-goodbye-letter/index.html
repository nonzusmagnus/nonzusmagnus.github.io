Flynn Johnson — Visual Treatment

A Goodbye Letter for the World

Location: Whitestown  ·  Artist: Flynn Johnson  ·  Concept Draft: v1

A memory being revisited. Warm and heavy at the same time. Not a protest. Not a tragedy. The world exhaling — laces on a wire, kids on a street, a burning car with flowers growing out of it.

Core Motifs

Throughline

The Laces

  • 01
    Opening: Flynn's hand in close-up, holding the laces. The first image. Nothing else yet.
  • 02
    The throw: Slow-motion shot of a pair being thrown up onto the wire. The ritual of it. Satisfying and melancholic at once.
  • 03
    Multiple pairs: Wide shot of the wire — not just one pair. Several. Each suggesting a different person, a different story hanging up there.
  • 04
    Time passage: Same shot of the laces across different light — dawn, overcast, golden hour. Passage of time without a word.
  • 05
    Flynn on the wire: Close-up of his hand gripping the telephone wire. Knuckles. Tension. Hanging on or letting go — the edit decides.
  • 06
    Birds on the wire: Wide shot — birds sitting alongside the laces. Something startles them and they scatter. The laces stay.
  • 07
    Final frame: Just the laces. No Flynn. No crew. Wind moving them slightly. The world quieted out.
Literal Thread

The Letter

  • 01
    Handwritten page: Camera drifts slowly across a letter. Words visible but not fully readable. Intimate without being exposing.
  • 02
    Burning paper: A letter held as it catches flame at the corner. Not dramatic — quiet. Someone letting something go.
  • 03
    Left behind: Someone folding a letter and leaving it somewhere in Whitestown — tucked into a railing, placed on a wall. Unanswered.
Innocence

The Kids

  • 01
    The fight: Little cousins going at it. Real, not choreographed. Let it breathe.
  • 02
    After the fight: Two minutes later they're laughing again. The resilience of kids. The fact that they shake it off in a way adults can't.
  • 03
    The observer: One kid sitting alone, watching the others play. Not involved. Just watching. That stillness carries weight.
  • 04
    The smokebud: A kid picks up a flower from the grass and looks at it. Curiosity against the backdrop of decay.
  • 05
    Hand sequence: Close-up of a kid's hands → Flynn's hands → the telephone wire. Connecting generations through the same gesture.
Location

Whitestown

  • 01
    Wide establishing: The estate in full. Not aestheticised — honest. Let it be what it is.
  • 02
    Empty playground: Swings moving with no one on them. Wind doing the work.
  • 03
    The wall: Old faded graffiti. Layers of who was here before. Time written into the surface.
  • 04
    People walking: Background life — not performing for camera. A woman hanging washing. Someone walking a dog. The video happens around them.
  • 05
    Street light: Shot at dusk or dawn. A single street light flickering in and out. Understated unease.
  • 06
    Window reflection: Flynn or another subject seen through a window — the outside world reflected over their face. Inside and outside layered.
Destruction & Growth

The Burning Car

  • 01
    The puddle: Reflection of the flames in standing water on the ground — the fire inverted, softened, distorted.
  • 02
    Flowers from the wreck: Flowers growing directly out of the car's bonnet — not just nearby grass. Growth from the thing that's burning.
  • 03
    The watcher: Someone standing beside the burning car, calm. Not panicked. Just watching. Normalised destruction.
  • 04
    The bicycle: A kid's bike leaning against the burning car. Nobody comes to get it.
  • 05
    Smokebuds close-up: Flowers in the grass that look like smoke rising. The estate's own kind of bloom.
The Break

"Disadvantaged"

  • 01
    The hold: Person holding the sign — they don't look at camera. They stare ahead. Quiet dignity, not protest.
  • 02
    Multiple holders: Different people — different ages — taking turns holding it in the same spot. Community as a shared condition.
  • 03
    Weathered: The sign slightly worn, damp. As if it's been there a long time. Because it has.
  • 04
    The exit: They put it down and walk away. No statement. Just departure. The sign stays in frame after they've gone.
Extra Visual Pieces

Additional Shots

  • 01
    Clock or watch: Stopped, or ticking slowly. Time as a quiet theme — never heavy-handed, just present.
  • 02
    Someone laughing: Cut before we find out why. Warmth the viewer can't fully access.
  • 03
    People walking: A slow stream of people moving through Whitestown — not performing, just existing. Shot wide, from a distance.
  • 04
    Chrisendo cameo: Present at the end, staying on the laces. His presence anchors the video's close.
Direction

Tone

This should feel like a memory being revisited, warm and heavy at the same time. The burning car, the kids playing, the smokebuds, the laces, on the same afternoon.

The "Disadvantaged" sign is the only moment that breaks the fourth wall. Everything else is part of the aesthetic.

Production

Shoot Notes

Chrisendo
On the laces throughout every significant section. The laces are the edit's anchor, his presence enforces that.
Light
Overcast. Expect flat grey skies. Avoid harsh midday, it strips the intimacy.
Camera Kids
Handheld. Loose and real. Let the energy come to the camera, don't chase it.
Camera Whitestown
Locked off for wide establishing shots. The contrast in camera movement mirrors the contrast in content.
Shoot Target
Sunday 13 Sep or Monday 22 Sep. Call pending to confirm scope, pricing and deposit.
Performance
Minimal or none, no rapping into camera. Flynn present as a figure within the world, not performing to the viewer.
